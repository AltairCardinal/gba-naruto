limitations

The PSG frequency lookup artifact classifies exactly 226 frozen bytes: 154 bytes in two bounded Thumb code ranges, twelve bytes in three consumed pointer literals, and a 60-byte channel-4 noise register descriptor table. The two-byte 0x09B4C6..0x09B4C8 alignment gap remains unknown. Entry proof is limited to one fixed initializer-to-logical-SoundInfo+0x30-to-BX-r3 chain; global RAM/computed producers, writers, indirect callers, natural runtime reachability, the two non-noise target-table semantics, playback, editing, writeback, relocation, and allocatable space remain unclaimed.
